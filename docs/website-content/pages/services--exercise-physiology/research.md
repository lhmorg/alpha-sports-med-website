## Retain / change / remove
Retain: listening first, health and activity history, goals, functional assessment where appropriate, personalised exercise programme, collaborative team and Newport supervised exercise space. These align with playbook and physio/osteo practical next-step positioning.
Change: legacy brief's pain-stopped/strength-gap concept into an individual planning discussion. Pain and capacity are not a universal explanation for recurrence. EP need not be a mandatory late phase or follow discharge. Explain overlap with physiotherapy without fixed job boundaries; add ordinary daily activity/chronic-health readers as well as sport.
Remove: prevention of re-injury, 'most clinics stop', guaranteed root-cause discovery, 'the reason your injury keeps returning', superior-to-PT language, comparative superiority, clinical testimonials, fixed recovery sequences/timelines. No evidence pack substantiates these.
