## explainer.heading

What does strength and conditioning involve?

## explainer.paragraphs[0]

Strength and conditioning involves planning exercise for the demands of an activity. That can include choosing strength exercises, deciding how much work to do and allowing for recovery in your week. The details depend on your goals and starting point.

## explainer.paragraphs[1]

Clinical rehabilitation considers symptoms and health needs. Coaching focuses on organising training. These roles can overlap. Your assessment helps determine which approach suits you.

## explainer.paragraphs[2]

If you need clinical exercise assessment, <a href="/services/exercise-physiology/">exercise physiology</a> may also be relevant. Your practitioner can help you consider which appointment fits the question you are trying to answer.

## faqHeading

Strength & Conditioning: your questions

## faqs[0].question

How do I book strength and conditioning?

## faqs[0].answer

The current booking flow does not have a separate strength and conditioning category. Call reception on 03 9962 5539 to choose an appropriate assessment, or discuss exercise programming with your existing practitioner.

## faqs[1].question

Do I have to be an existing patient?

## faqs[1].answer

Call reception to explain what you are looking for. The team can help you choose a starting assessment rather than assuming a particular programme or appointment is right for you.

## faqs[2].question

How is this different from personal training?

## faqs[2].answer

The conversation at Alpha can take place in the context of musculoskeletal assessment and rehabilitation. Personal trainers offer varied services and qualifications. Check the role and scope of anyone you work with, particularly when you have symptoms or an injury.

## faqs[3].question

Do I need access to a gym?

## faqs[3].answer

Tell your practitioner what space and equipment you have available. Exercise choices can be discussed in that context; the plan needs to fit what you have.

## faqs[4].question

How soon can I return to sport?

## faqs[4].answer

That depends on your injury or health circumstances and the demands of the sport. Your treating team should guide return decisions. A return date needs to be discussed with them after assessment.

## faqs[5].question

Can I claim a rebate for coaching?

## faqs[5].answer

Coaching does not automatically qualify for an allied health rebate. Eligibility depends on the actual service, practitioner and your cover or funding arrangements. Confirm the appointment and fee with reception and any insurer before attending.
