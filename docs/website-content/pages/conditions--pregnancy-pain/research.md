## Clinical primary sources
https://www.thewomens.org.au/patients-visitors/clinics-and-services/support-services/physiotherapy : Royal Women's Hospital describes individual assessment and its pregnancy pelvic girdle pain resource.
https://www.thewomens.org.au/images/uploads/fact-sheets/Pregnancy-related-pelvic-girdle-pain-210319.pdf : hospital factsheet supports practical activity modification, support options and physiotherapy-guided exercises; published 2019 so avoid treating every recommendation as personalised/current prescription.
https://www.pregnancybirthbaby.org.au/pregnancy/pregnancy-stages/first-trimester : current government-supported source says bleeding or abdominal pain in first trimester needs medical advice; do not normalise these as routine muscle pain.
https://www.pregnancybirthbaby.org.au/pregnancy/health-conditions-and-complications/warning-signs-during-pregnancy : warning-sign resource current indexed, redirected direct text not retrievable. Reviewer should verify final red-flag wording from primary source.
https://www.pregnancybirthbaby.org.au/labour-and-birth/labour/giving-birth-early-signs-of-labour : urgent heavy bleeding/severe headache/blurred vision pathway includes 000; any concern contact maternity care. Avoid PANDA as physical-emergency pathway.

## Draft decisions
Retain: practical turning in bed/stairs/car symptoms, back and pelvic girdle pain, individual musculoskeletal assessment and movement support. Common does not mean ignore; clinical assessment distinguishes pregnancy concerns.
Change: explain maternity care first for new worrying symptoms. Distinct urgent block immediately following hero. Seek prompt maternity advice for bleeding, fluid loss, reduced/changed baby movement, severe headache/visual change and severe abdominal pain; emergency 000 for severe symptoms. Final wording requires independent healthcare review.
Omit: universal safety, exact trimester-specific manipulation ban as Alpha policy unless confirmed, internal pelvic-floor assessment, birth outcome claims, pain definitely resolves after birth, unverified prevalence, all-clinic/all-discipline scope.
Support exercise conditional on maternity guidance and individual restrictions; no prescription or clearance online. No implied treatment for round-ligament pain/obstetric disease.
