## explainer.heading

What is Clinical Pilates?

## explainer.paragraphs[0]

Pilates is a form of exercise that can use a mat or equipment. A reformer has a moving carriage and spring resistance, which can be adjusted for different exercises. You do not need to know the equipment before asking about a session.

## explainer.paragraphs[1]

Alpha offers one-to-one reformer sessions. Your health history and goals inform the exercises you practise. This individual appointment format gives you room to discuss symptoms, ask questions and change the pace.

## explainer.paragraphs[2]

The <a href="https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/pilates-health-benefits">Victorian Better Health Channel Pilates guide</a> explains the different formats and the value of advice when you have a health condition, are pregnant or have recently had surgery.

## faqHeading

Clinical Pilates: your questions

## faqs[0].question

Do I need Pilates experience?

## faqs[0].answer

You can ask about a session without having used a reformer before. Tell the clinician about your exercise experience so the guidance can start at an appropriate level.

## faqs[1].question

Are these group classes?

## faqs[1].answer

Alpha offers one-to-one reformer Clinical Pilates sessions in Newport. Ask reception about the current appointment options.

## faqs[2].question

How does it compare with a studio class?

## faqs[2].answer

Alpha offers an individual appointment informed by your health history and goals. Studio formats vary, so check what is included with the provider you are considering.

## faqs[3].question

Can I do Clinical Pilates while pregnant?

## faqs[3].answer

Discuss exercise with your maternity team and tell the clinician about your pregnancy and any restrictions. Suitability and exercise choice need individual assessment; Pilates is not automatically appropriate for every pregnancy.

## faqs[4].question

What if I have recently had surgery?

## faqs[4].answer

Follow your treating team’s instructions and let us know about the procedure and any restrictions. The clinician may need further information before deciding which exercises are appropriate.

## faqs[5].question

Do I need a referral?

## faqs[5].answer

You can enquire about a private appointment without a referral. Bring relevant advice from your treating team. Funding arrangements can have separate requirements.

## faqs[6].question

Can I use private health insurance?

## faqs[6].answer

Eligibility depends on your cover, the service and the practitioner providing it. Ask reception and your insurer before attending. A Pilates appointment does not automatically attract a rebate.

## faqs[7].question

How many sessions will I need?

## faqs[7].answer

That depends on your goals and how you respond. Discuss a review point with the clinician rather than assuming a fixed number of sessions.
