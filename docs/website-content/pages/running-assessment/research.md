# Research: Running assessment
Research date: 6 October 2026. Read required project inputs, running specialty brief, playbook/profile. Root route /running-assessment/ must remain. Local draft only.

## Verified clinic product
https://alphasportsmed.com.au/running-assessment/ (direct official page retrieved).
The service is osteopathy-led. It describes evaluating running form, gait and biomechanics, using video analysis, and creating an individual treatment/rehab plan. It serves both runners with pain and recreational runners wanting assessment. Newport is explicitly located; additional copy alternates Ascot Vale, Maidstone and Footscray. These inconsistent names are service-area language, not proof of present equipment or appointment sites. Do not claim Footscray/Maidstone clinics. Generic own-page treatment examples include joint mobilisation, soft tissue and exercise when indicated.
Remove prevention, PB/speed, efficiency, faster recovery and state-of-the-art superiority claims. Remove yearly assessment recommendation and urgency suggesting every runner needs paid assessment.
No price, duration, treadmill, camera angles, software, fatigue protocol, written report entitlement, follow-up inclusion or rebate is verified. Proposed brief's 60–75 minutes and $200 comparison are hypothetical and MUST NOT become copy. Video analysis itself IS verified; don't overcorrect by claiming it is unknown.

## Evidence-backed positioning
https://alphasportsmed.com.au/staff/dr-lara-trevorrow/ (official indexed result; direct timeout): Lara treats running-related injuries as an osteopath and has sporting interests. Not proof she delivers dedicated running assessments; do not name her as product provider.
Playbook supports listening, education and goals, but does not substantiate identifying a singular root cause or keeping everyone running. Avoid promising rest is rarely necessary. Stopping running can be appropriate; discuss assessment and personalised load decisions. No outcome claim needs to be used for an informative service page.

## Draft decisions
Retain: video-informed gait assessment, osteopath-led care, individual plan, injured AND uninjured runners, current shoe/training-information preparation framed as useful to bring rather than a strict clinic protocol.
Change: explain running pattern is only one part of understanding symptoms; history and discussion of current training are sensible process framing, not invented exhaustive laboratory protocol. Technology description must stay simply video analysis. Ask to confirm appointment format, fee and location before booking; do not publish an invented numeric price to satisfy old brief.
Omit: three camera angles, treadmill, multiple paces/fatigue trials, clinical motion capture, guaranteed written report, orthotic/shoe sales independence, exact testing battery, annual assessment schedule, injury prediction/prevention and performance claims.
