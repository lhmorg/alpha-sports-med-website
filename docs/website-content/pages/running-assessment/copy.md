## explainer.heading

Running assessment and video analysis

## explainer.paragraphs[0]

Video analysis provides a way to look at running movement, including gait and technique. The osteopath can discuss observations with you and consider them alongside your symptoms and circumstances.

## explainer.paragraphs[1]

A video is one piece of information. It cannot establish every cause of pain or predict whether you will be injured. Your history and clinical assessment matter when considering what to do next.

## explainer.paragraphs[2]

The aim is an individual plan informed by the assessment. That may involve a discussion about exercise or treatment when appropriate. Ask what the appointment includes and how any follow-up would be arranged.

## faqHeading

Running assessment: your questions

## faqs[0].question

Do I need to have an injury?

## faqs[0].answer

You can enquire about an assessment without an injury. Tell reception what you want to understand about your running so they can help you choose an appropriate appointment.

## faqs[1].question

What can video analysis tell me?

## faqs[1].answer

It can show features of your running movement for discussion with the osteopath. It is considered alongside your history and assessment; it cannot identify every cause of symptoms or predict future injuries.

## faqs[2].question

Do I need to run pain-free on the day?

## faqs[2].answer

Tell the osteopath about your symptoms before running. They can consider what assessment is appropriate. You should not assume you need to push through pain to complete an appointment.

## faqs[3].question

Will I be told to stop running?

## faqs[3].answer

Advice depends on the assessment. Some situations call for modifying or stopping an activity. Ask the osteopath to explain any recommendation and how it fits your circumstances.

## faqs[4].question

How long does it take and what does it cost?

## faqs[4].answer

Contact reception on 03 9962 5539 to confirm the current duration, fee and appointment format before booking.

## faqs[5].question

Will I receive a report or a follow-up?

## faqs[5].answer

Ask what is included in the appointment and how the plan will be provided. Check with reception whether a written report or follow-up is included.

## faqs[6].question

Can I claim a rebate?

## faqs[6].answer

Eligibility depends on the actual appointment, practitioner and your cover or funding arrangements. Check with reception and your insurer before attending.

## faqs[7].question

What should I bring?

## faqs[7].answer

Your usual running shoes and recent training information may be useful. Ask reception about clothing and any specific preparation for the appointment.
