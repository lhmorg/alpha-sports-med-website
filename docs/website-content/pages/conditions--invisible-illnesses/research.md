Preserve recognition and collaboration concepts.
I2 https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/chapter/recommendations — official current published ME/CFS guideline, especially 1.11.9–1.11.14. General exercise advice may worsen symptoms. Exercise programme requires specialist-team oversight, patient choice, individual energy limits; fixed incremental increases and deconditioning-based programmes must not be offered. Exercise does not cure ME/CFS. This is UK clinical guidance used to assess safety/evidence, not Australian clinic accreditation or law. Alpha ME/CFS specialist expertise/oversight is unverified; do not advertise clinic ME/CFS exercise treatment or 'rebuild capacity' to this whole population.
I3 https://www.ehlers-danlos.com/diagnosis/ — hEDS/HSD distinct diagnoses, symptom presentations need appropriate medical assessment. Do not funnel unexplained illness into presumed hypermobility.
I4 https://alphasportsmed.com.au/hypermobility/ — verified local service pathway with explicit hEDS/HSD medical diagnosis boundary, movement assessment and collaborative care. It is appropriate to link for readers who already have hypermobility concerns; not assert cause of their unrelated symptoms.

## Retain / change / remove
Retain calm recognition that appearances do not measure illness and that daily activity/appointments can cost energy. 'Invisible illness' is descriptive umbrella, not a diagnosis. Explain Alpha's musculoskeletal role clearly, discuss suitability before booking, support coordination with existing GP/specialists with consent. Individual support may be appropriate for specific musculoskeletal concerns; do not prescribe generic graded activity to everyone.
Change transactional condition-treatment page into scope/trust information. Avoid implying normal scans mean a particular unseen diagnosis. Use diverse readers, not assumed female identity. No sweeping claim every member experiences boom-and-bust or was disbelieved; conditional recognition is more truthful. Symptom worsening after exertion is not merely ordinary deconditioning; accommodate rest/limits and specialist advice.
Remove guarantees of validation/outcome, 'regulate your nervous system', treating underlying autoimmune/endocrine/neurologic disease, curing lipedema, fatigue reversal, detox/supplement promises, treatment scope inferred from long lists. Do not advertise diagnosis of fibromyalgia/ME/CFS/POTS/endometriosis/autoimmune disease. No specialist/expert title.
