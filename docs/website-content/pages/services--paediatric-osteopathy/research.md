## Primary evidence and boundaries
https://osteopathy.org.au/Web/Advocacy/Position-Statement-pages/Osteopathic-Clinical-Management-for-Babies-Infants-and-Children.aspx (full primary professional position retrieved; updated 2025).
Osteopathy Australia recommends against spinal manipulative techniques under 12 because systematic benefit evidence is limited. It describes age-appropriate musculoskeletal assessment, consent and referral precautions and possible soft-tissue, positioning, play and exercise approaches. Do not equate gentle with proven safe or effective. Explain proposed care and risks before consent; parent/carer involvement.
https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Resources/Advertising-hub/Resources-for-advertisers/Osteopathy-examples (official search result retrieved; direct 403). Separate compliance reviewer must inspect current guidance; unsupported non-musculoskeletal claims and specialist implication must be excluded.
