## Source ledger
S1 https://alphasportsmed.com.au/sporting-injuries/ direct fetch timed out; current search index supplied page (crawl last month). Clinic supports injury assessment, manual options and exercise rehabilitation for active people; acute/overuse injury categories, return-to-sport planning and referral needs. Existing page claims faster recovery, preventing re-injury and root-cause certainty: do not salvage. Its unrelated back-pain FAQs and old phone are errors, not authority.
S2 https://alphasportsmed.com.au/gym-injuries/ indexed current clinic source. Preserve gym context: pain can interfere with lifts/training, review training history and adapt loads/movements. Remove simplistic causation ('poor technique' as explanation for all injury), guaranteed safe/full return and prevention promises.
S3 https://alphasportsmed.com.au/soft-tissue-injuries/ indexed current clinic source. Preserve muscle, tendon/ligament examples and tailored rehabilitation. Remove faster-recovery/prevention guarantees and fixed protocol claims. No full salvage of education-hub strain/rest articles performed: topics retained with independent official definitions.
S4 https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/health/conditionsandtreatments/sprains-and-strains/ — Victorian government primary patient guidance. Supports protection/relative rest after acute soft-tissue injury, then appropriate activity, variable healing. Do not turn 'rest is not always best' into advice to train through acute injury.
S5 https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/amp/article/sprains-and-strains and https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/limb-injuries — Australian government-funded guidance verified. Sprain concerns ligaments, strain muscle/tendon. Assess severe pain, suspected fracture/dislocation and worsening symptoms medically. Deformity/inability to weight bear require medical assessment; numb/pale/cold limb is ambulance 000 advice. Keep concise red flags and signposting, no remote triage.
S6 https://www.ais.gov.au/media-centre/news/concussion_in_sport_australia_launches_toolkit_and_position_statement — official AIS source supports recognise/remove suspected concussion. This retrieved announcement is older; do not use it for current return-day counts. Clinic expertise/medical clearance and current concussion protocol unverified: remove concussion service promise, direct head knocks to medical assessment, no same-day return.
Local Ashton practitioner collection maps sports injuries/Newport with portrait/profile but empty media. Existing physio page supports criteria-discussion rather than dates. Osteo/physio process reference is layout/voice, not clinical outcome evidence.

## Retain / change / remove
Retain acute versus gradually developing symptoms, sport/gym/soft-tissue entry points, assessment first, useful sprain/strain explanation, individually discussed changes and goal/review points. Separate injury hub from sports-physio service intent.
Change legacy brief's 'the fix is almost never stop' to assessment-dependent modification/offloading, no fixed duration, no performance wording. Four business clinics in profile supersede three-business-clinic assumption; service appointments depend on practitioner/location, do not guarantee all disciplines everywhere. No 45-minute universal visit, same-week availability or health fund promise without confirmation.
Remove prevention claims, faster/better-than-alternatives, misalignment/root-cause guarantee, clearance-to-play promise and trauma care marketing. Time alone does not determine readiness, but criteria differ by injury; avoid universal pass/fail formula.
