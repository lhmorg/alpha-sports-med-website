Pattern: sudden injury, gradually building symptoms, gym-related discomfort, returning after injury. Distinguish sprain ligaments and strain muscle/tendon from government guidance, examples not diagnosis. No poor-technique single-cause blame.

Process: understand incident/training, screen referral needs, assess appropriate movement, explain options, agree manageable plan, review activity goals. No specific testing battery/numbers or guaranteed same-week access.
Note: early guidance after acute soft-tissue injury individual, protection/relative rest then appropriate activity; don't supply rigid online rehab protocol. Progression differs between injuries; treating clinician/medical clearance where needed.
